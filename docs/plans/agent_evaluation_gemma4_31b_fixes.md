## What the fixes changed

- **Tie lines** (13, 14, 18). Gemma used one `control_area` qualifier per area at once, in two calls, and
  each question took about 3 minutes instead of 18 to 26. Question 14's "no" now rests on the right query,
  and question 18 covers all 25 ties.
- **Thermal counts** (10, 15, 20, 26). The counts no longer include a line's own outage. Question 20 found 26
  new and 12 resolved overloads, the reference counts, where it had found 241 and 159.
- **Confirmation** (22). `configure_run` returned a preview; gemma showed the diff and asked, and the XML was
  unchanged.
- **Joins** (5). Generation by area came from one joined call and matches the reference in every area.
- **Unknown values** (8). A qualifier on status listed the 252 failed contingencies directly.
- **Scripts** (23, 24). The guarded run's script used the right paths and the Parquet index. It failed on
  the 1 GiB memory limit because it loaded every case at once, and question 24 reported that failure as one.

## What is still wrong

- **Question 23, before `a5e3a00`, showed a new failure the tie-line fix made easy.** Gemma summed `p_from_mw`
  over the 25 ties and called −1,697.6 MW the minimum net flow. Each tie's flow is signed at its own from
  end, 9 in Far West and 16 in West, so the sum mixed directions; the oriented minimum is 2,945.40 MW. Since
  `a5e3a00`, `rank_groups` warns, and the controller adds a note, whenever it totals signed flows across
  branches. Gemma then wrote a script instead.
- **Question 12 still claims MVA headroom.** Gemma gave "spare capacity of approximately 152.8 MVA" from
  base-case loading. The prompt forbids it, and the controller's note is appended, but the claim stays in the
  answer.
- **Omitted caveats keep most partial scores partial**: the in-service filter (5), the count of islanded
  outages (8), the worst single case (10), converged-only scope (15, stated in the first run but not here),
  and the network limit (23).
- **A qualifier on the circuit field still fails silently.** Question 12 matched a line's name against
  `line_id`, which holds circuit IDs, and got zero rows with no warning. The value check covers bus and event
  numbers, not circuits.
- **Scripts are still fragile.** The script loaded 74.7 million rows at once and misread the RAW bus section.
  The description now shows the streaming pattern, but the model did not follow it.

Each prompt was asked once, so a single changed verdict, such as question 15's, may be the model's run-to-run
variation rather than an effect of the fixes.
